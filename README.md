# World's Economy Analysis

This project presents an analysis and key insights derived from four integrated datasets using Python's data stack:
- Global energy supply indicators
- Historical GDP data from the World Bank
- Global inflation statistics
- Journal & Country Rank data for Energy Engineering and Power Technology

---

## 📑 Table of Contents
- Data Cleaning
- Average Gross Domestic Product (GDP)
- Regional Comparison: GDP Distribution by Continent
- GDP vs. Population Relationship
- Impact of Renewable Energies on Economic Growth
- Emerging vs. Developed Economies: Comparative Overview
- Statistical Comparison of GDP between Emerging and Developed Nations
- Trends in Renewable Energy Adoption
- Inflation and GDP Interrelation
- Inflation Rate Tendencies Over Time
